## Functional ADPCM stream checkpoint

word addresses, advances eight words per block with RAM wrap, retains predictor
history, records encoded loop starts unless explicitly overridden, repeats on
END+LOOP and stops on nonrepeating END. Invalid predictors and inactive streams

not connected to guest key-on registers or a mixer. Its encountered_end flag is
not a cycle-accurate ENDX event, and next_word_address is not hardware NAX.
