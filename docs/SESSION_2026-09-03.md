## Guard-free selector hardening

Review before executable trace emission found that a highly biased conditional
branch still has two statically possible successors and therefore cannot be
called guard-free. The direct selector now requires exactly one static successor
before chaining blocks. Conditional traces remain stopped at that boundary until
the emitter consumes the guard/deopt contract above.
