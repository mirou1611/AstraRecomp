## Strongest correctness result

The Phase A3 census identified IOP `0x00097500 -> 0x1F900744` with 222,884
16-bit reads. Decoding the adjacent BIOS routine showed that `0x1F900744` is
SPU2 core 1 STATX and that the BIOS waits for ready bit `0x0080`. The old model
left STATX at zero and exhausted a `1 << 24` software timeout.

After modeling ready/busy transitions:

- the 222,884-read timeout disappears;
- total IOP MMIO reads fall from 355,171 to 140,236;
- SIF0 activity transitions rise from 434 to 452;
- IOP blocks rise from 5,006 to 5,016 and edges from 6,896 to 6,922;
- SIF1 DMA starts rise from 86 to 95;
- SPU2 DMA7 starts rise from two to five;
- the final sampled IOP PC moves from `0x000974F8` to `0x00093F10`.

The EE remains running. No VIF0, VIF1, or GIF DMA start is observed, so this is
boot/device progress rather than graphics progress.
