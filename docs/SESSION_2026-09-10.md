## VU1 pending division at program termination

Fixed pending Q retirement after the E-bit delay pair completes. Previously a
microprogram could stop with stale Q, leaving its division pending until a later
execution step. Host instruction-budget exhaustion still does not drain Q.
Program-end drain cycles are included in the existing Q stall counter. This is
only the modeled division pipeline, not a complete hardware pipeline flush.

Regression tests cover DIV in the E-marked pair and DIV in its delay pair,
single-pair host budgets, final Q/cycle/pair counts, and a resumed WAITQ that must
not wait again for the already-retired division.

Reference behavior checked in PCSX2 v2.4.0:

- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PCSX2/pcsx2/v2.4.0/pcsx2/VU1microInterp.cpp
  invokes pipeline flush when the E-bit delay completes.
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PCSX2/pcsx2/v2.4.0/pcsx2/VUops.cpp
  `_vuFlushAll` retires pending Q and advances to division completion.
