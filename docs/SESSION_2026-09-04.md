# Session 2026-09-04: VU1 continuation bring-up

## Outcome

The first VU1 `MSCAL` program remains validated at 55 executed pairs, two
queued path-1 GIF tags, and a clean E-bit stop at TPC `0xD8`. `MSCNT` now
continues from that retained TPC, while the VIF frontend models BASE, OFFSET,
TOP/TOPS double buffering, ITOP state, and top-relative V4-32 UNPACK placement.

The real 248.8-million-step BIOS replay accepts the full VIF stream with zero
rejected packets and unpacks 124 vectors. Successive evidence runs advanced
the continuation frontier as follows:

```
55 -> 59 pairs: MTIR, then IBNE at 0x0F8
59 -> 68 pairs: IBNE/XTOP/ILWR/IAND, then LQ at 0x140
68 -> 91 pairs: LQ/SQ and address setup, then DIV at 0x1F8
91 -> 95 pairs: DIV/WAITQ/MULq, then MAXx at 0x218
95 -> 100 pairs: MAX/SUB and signed IADDI, then FMAND at 0x240
100 -> 107 pairs: prior-pair MAC flags/FMAND, then FTOI0 at 0x2B0
```

The current interpreter includes exact functional implementations and focused
tests for those operations plus masked vector ADD/SUB/MUL/MAX broadcast forms,
MFIR, all four FTOI scale variants, unconditional B, and signed IBLEZ used by
the captured program. Branch and E delay pairs are explicit.

The apparent stall at the FTOI block was a finite transform loop rather than
an unsupported opcode. Its VI10 counter fell from approximately `0x8004` to
`0x736B` during a 100,000-pair diagnostic slice. Raising the guarded per-command
execution budget to two million pairs allowed the complete VIF workload to
finish: VI10 reached zero, VU1 stopped cleanly at TPC `0x308`, and no lower or
upper opcode remained unsupported. Across its MSCAL/MSCNT calls, the BIOS ran
6,095,819 VU1 pairs and queued 68 path-1 GIF tags.

The first GIF replay at this frontier exposed an important streaming error:
the `0x1000`-qword IMAGE transfer spans multiple DMA submissions, but the old
parser treated every submission as a self-contained packet. It rejected 26
apparently truncated packets and decoded raw image words as later tags. The
GIF frontend now retains an incomplete tag and payload across submissions,
traverses complete IMAGE/IMAGE2 payloads, honors tag PRE/PRIM state, and
assembles point, line, triangle-list/strip/fan, and sprite primitives.

The corrected replay receives the same 96 GIF submissions with zero rejection:
83 packed tags, 13 image tags, and 368,640 raw image bytes. The old 1,825 sprite
count was also shown to be a PRE/PRIM misclassification; the real stream emits
two sprites before broader primitive/local-memory handling. The framebuffer is
still hash `AE6BFFA9F3064B83` with `0/17920` nonzero pixels, so the next frontier
is GS host-to-local image storage and display/framebuffer selection rather than
GIF packet validity. This is major graphics-pipeline progress, but not yet a
visible PS2 intro.

Transfer-state instrumentation confirms that all 13 uploads are coherent
host-to-local asset surfaces. They use the observed PSMCT32 and PSMCT16 formats,
destination bases from `0x3200` through `0x3760`, and 64x64 through 256x256
rectangles. Their declared dimensions exactly account for all 368,640 payload
bytes. The first transfer is a 256x64 PSMCT32 surface at base `0x3200` with
DBW 4. A heap-backed 4 MiB logical GS local-memory oracle now stores both
observed formats with DBP/DBW/TRXPOS/TRXREG addressing; a focused 2x2 PSMCT32
test validates row stride and pixel order. Texture sampling and display-buffer
selection are the next session boundary.
